## `GET /v1/compliance/report/{entity_id}` withdrawn

The endpoint returned the same EU AI Act briefing for every `entity_id`, so it was not the
per-entity assessment it was priced as. It now answers `410 Gone` with `errorCode`
`compliance_report_unavailable`, is no longer priced or listed in discovery, and a call to
it is not charged.

For an EU AI Act readiness score from inputs you supply, use
`GET /v1/compliance/readiness/{entity_id}`. For a per-entity report built from a real
check, use `POST /v1/reports/entity` (see [September 24](/changelog/2026-09-24)).
