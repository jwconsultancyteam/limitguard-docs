## Lead Verify

`POST /v1/leads/verify` checks one Dutch or Belgian sales lead in one call: is it a real,
active company in the KVK or KBO register, and do its VAT number, mail server, IBAN country
and name hold up, including a check against the OFAC, EU and UN sanctions lists. It
returns a verdict, a 0-100 lead score and flags. It costs $0.24. Over MCP it is the tool
`verify_lead`.

<Note>
  Lead Verify has cost $0.27 since [October 4](/changelog/2026-10-04).
</Note>

## Agent wallet check

`POST /v1/agent/check` checks a counterparty agent's EVM wallet in one call: a match
against the OFAC SDN digital-currency address list, its Base USDC and ETH balance, its
ERC-8004 identity registration and open ERC-8004 feedback, which is not scored. It costs
$0.75. Over MCP it is the tool `check_agent_wallet`.

## `get_trust_score` returns

The MCP tool `get_trust_score`, removed on 24 September because it returned placeholder
data, is back as a real lookup: your own most recent stored trust score for an entity you
checked before, with when, which product and the trend. It runs no new check and is not
charged.
