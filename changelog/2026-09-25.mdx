## Compliance alerts are priced

`GET /v1/compliance/alerts`, the feed of daily OFAC, EU and UN sanctions-list changes that
match what you checked, costs $0.10 per call over x402 or from a key's balance. The
dashboard's Growth, Pro and Enterprise plans include it.

<Note>
  The feed has cost $0.11 since [October 5](/changelog/2026-10-05).
</Note>

## `identifier.change.new` webhooks

Watchlist entries are now re-checked weekly on the key tiers that include monitoring, and
a change found in one of an entry's signals (for example registry status, VAT validity,
insolvency, legal form or domain expiry) is sent as an `identifier.change.new` webhook. See [Webhooks](/guides/webhooks).

## `get_compliance_report` over MCP

The per-entity report is now also the MCP tool `get_compliance_report`, at the price of
`POST /v1/reports/entity`.

## Group structure in KYB

`POST /v1/kyb/check` and the entity report now include `group_structure` from GLEIF: the
company's LEI and its direct and ultimate parent, when GLEIF links them.
